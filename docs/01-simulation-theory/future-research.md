# Purpose

This document outlines long-term research directions for the Simulation Engine.

The purpose is not to define committed roadmap items.

Instead, it identifies promising research areas that may significantly improve the platform over time.

The Simulation Engine should evolve continuously as new research, technologies, behavioral science, and customer feedback become available.

This document intentionally contains ambitious ideas.

Many may never be implemented.

Others may fundamentally redefine the platform.

---

# Research Philosophy

The objective of research is not feature expansion.

The objective is improving our understanding of digital human behavior.

Every research initiative should improve one or more of the following:

Prediction Quality

Explainability

Calibration

Scalability

Behavioral Realism

Scientific Rigor

Business Value

# Grand Challenges

Long-term scientific challenges include

Predicting behavior over months rather than minutes

Modeling organizational decision making

Understanding trust formation across cultures

Learning continuously without catastrophic forgetting

Building explainable causal models of UX

Generating scientifically valid synthetic populations

Learning from sparse real-world feedback

Scaling simulations to millions of concurrent users

These challenges are expected to require years of research.

# Success Criteria

The research program succeeds when the platform becomes

More accurate

More explainable

More efficient

More scientifically grounded

More broadly applicable

More valuable for product teams

Research should improve the platform without sacrificing transparency.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to build a general-purpose behavioral intelligence platform capable of understanding, simulating, and improving human interaction with digital systems.

The platform should evolve from evaluating isolated interfaces to understanding complete digital ecosystems.

Its purpose is not to replace human judgment.

Its purpose is to provide the highest-quality behavioral evidence possible before products reach real users.
