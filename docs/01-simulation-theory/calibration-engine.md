# Purpose

The Behavioral Learning & Calibration Engine continuously improves the Simulation Engine by comparing simulated behavior against observed real-world outcomes.

Its objective is not to eliminate prediction error.

Its objective is to measure prediction error, understand why it occurred, and update the platform so future simulations become more accurate.

Calibration transforms the Simulation Engine from a static prediction system into a continuously learning intelligence platform.

---

# Philosophy

Every prediction is a hypothesis.

Reality is the experiment.

The platform should continuously compare hypotheses with observed outcomes.

Learning never stops.

# Privacy

Calibration should prioritize customer privacy.

Personally identifiable information is never required.

Where possible, learning should use aggregated or anonymized behavioral statistics.

Customers should retain control over whether their data contributes to global learning.
