Design Philosophy

The engine is designed around five foundational principles:

Observe deeply

Collect every meaningful signal from a digital experience rather than relying on screenshots or HTML alone.

Simulate broadly

Model a wide spectrum of users with diverse goals, constraints, backgrounds, and behaviors.

Reason systematically

Separate observation, behavioral simulation, benchmarking, and recommendation into distinct stages with explicit responsibilities.

Explain clearly

Every insight should include supporting evidence, confidence, and reasoning that users can understand and evaluate.

Learn continuously

Improve simulation quality by comparing predictions with real-world outcomes whenever possible.
