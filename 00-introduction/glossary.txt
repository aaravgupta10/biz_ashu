Non-Goals

The engine is not intended to:

Guarantee conversion increases.
Replace real usability testing.
Replace analytics platforms.
Replace A/B testing.
Act as a generic chatbot.
Generate arbitrary opinions without evidence.
Optimize solely for engagement at the expense of user experience.
This is where I'll push you.

I think we should stop calling this a "user simulator."

That's one subsystem.

I think we're creating something much bigger.

I'd call it:

Predictive Product Intelligence Engine

The simulation engine is the heart.

But the product is about helping companies make better decisions before launch.

That positioning gives us room to expand into:

release readiness,
onboarding analysis,
pricing optimization,
UX benchmarking,
design validation,
accessibility,
and much more without changing our identity.
