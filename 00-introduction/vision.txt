Build the world's most trusted AI-powered human behavior simulation engine, enabling teams to understand, validate, and improve digital experiences before they ever reach real users.

We believe that every digital experience—from a startup landing page to enterprise software—should be stress-tested before launch. Today, businesses rely on analytics after users arrive, usability testing with small sample sizes, or generic AI feedback that lacks behavioral depth.

We envision a future where product teams can simulate thousands of realistic user journeys, uncover hidden friction, benchmark against industry standards, and receive evidence-backed recommendations before deploying changes.

Our long-term ambition is to establish a new category: Predictive Product Intelligence.

Instead of asking "What happened?" after launch, teams should be able to ask "What is likely to happen, why, and what should we improve before users arrive?"

Our simulation engine is designed not to replace real users, but to dramatically reduce uncertainty before products reach them.
