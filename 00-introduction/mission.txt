Mission

Help every product team make better decisions by transforming simulated human behavior into reliable, explainable, and actionable product intelligence.

Core Beliefs
Human behavior can never be predicted perfectly, but it can be modeled well enough to make better decisions than guessing.
AI should explain its reasoning, not merely produce answers.
Every recommendation should be supported by evidence and a confidence estimate.
Benchmarks and behavioral patterns are more valuable than isolated opinions.
Product reviews should evolve from subjective feedback into measurable, repeatable analysis.
The simulation engine must improve continuously as new data and validation become available.
Trust is earned through transparency, not certainty.
