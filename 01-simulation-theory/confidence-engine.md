# Purpose

The Evidence & Confidence Engine is responsible for evaluating the reliability of every observation, behavioral pattern, prediction, insight, and recommendation generated by the platform.

Its objective is not to determine whether an output is true.

Its objective is to estimate how strongly the available evidence supports that output.

Every user-facing conclusion must include both supporting evidence and a quantified estimate of confidence.

# Confidence Layers

The platform computes confidence at multiple levels.

---

## Layer 1

Observation Confidence

How certain are we that the observation itself is correct?

Examples

OCR certainty

Vision certainty

DOM extraction certainty

Semantic classification certainty

---

## Layer 2

Behavior Confidence

How consistently did simulations exhibit the behavior?

Examples

72% ignored CTA

Confidence

94%

---

## Layer 3

Aggregation Confidence

How stable is the observed population-level pattern?

Did

50 simulations agree?

500?

5,000?

50,000?

---

## Layer 4

Prediction Confidence

How likely is it that this behavioral pattern translates into real-world outcomes?

Prediction confidence incorporates

Calibration

Historical validation

Benchmark similarity

---

## Layer 5

Recommendation Confidence

How confident are we that the proposed recommendation addresses the underlying issue?

Multiple recommendations may explain the same problem.

Recommendation confidence estimates the likelihood that this recommendation is appropriate.

# Summary

The Evidence & Confidence Engine transforms raw observations into trustworthy intelligence by explicitly modeling evidence quality, agreement, uncertainty, and historical calibration.

Rather than presenting AI outputs as facts, the platform quantifies confidence through transparent, evidence-backed reasoning, allowing product teams to make informed decisions while understanding the limits of the system.
