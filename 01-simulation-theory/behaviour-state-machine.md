Purpose

This document defines the behavioral framework governing every synthetic human generated by the Simulation Engine.

Rather than relying on language models to improvise behavior, the engine follows a structured behavioral model inspired by cognitive psychology, behavioral economics, UX research, and decision theory.

The objective is not to perfectly reproduce individual human behavior.

The objective is to generate realistic populations whose collective behavior approximates meaningful patterns.
